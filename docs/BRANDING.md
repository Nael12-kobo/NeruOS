# NeruOS Branding Guide

## Name
- Product name: **NeruOS**
- Edition / visual identity: **Nebula**
- Preferred full form: **NeruOS — Nebula Edition**
- Public repository: **Nael12-kobo/NeruOS**

Use NeruOS for the project and operating-system name. Use Nebula for the first visual edition, theme, and design language.

## Logo concept

The mark is a flowing ribbon that suggests the letter **N**. It represents movement between ideas, creative flow, and a connected desktop ecosystem. The gradient moves from Nebula Cyan through blue and violet to Aurora Pink.

### Logo files

| File | Purpose |

## Clear space and sizing
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 20% of its visible height.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or recolor the gradient without documenting a new variant.
- Use the horizontal wordmark for wide headers and the mark alone for compact application icons.
- Keep the symbol large enough that the inner ribbon remains readable.

## Color palette
- Obsidian: #080F1A
- Midnight: #151D2F
- Nebula Cyan: #54C8EB
- Nebula Violet: #9277E8
- Aurora Pink: #E57BFF

## Voice and messaging

NeruOS speaks with confidence but does not overpromise. Describe working features as working, prototypes as prototypes, and future plans as future plans.

Preferred tagline: **Find your flow.**

Supporting line: **A fluid, powerful, and beautiful Linux experience.**

Avoid claims such as “runs every Windows app” or “plays every anti-cheat game.” Compatibility depends on upstream projects, publishers, drivers, and hardware.
